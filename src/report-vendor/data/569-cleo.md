## STAR stories

#### The split-payment marketplace

> Finturf needed a **split-payment marketplace** across twelve merchants. I owned the architecture from discovery through delivery — designed the Stripe Connect routing, built merchant onboarding workflows, and ran the compliance checks. It went live across **all 12 merchants** in one quarter and became the platform's core revenue engine.

_Reflection: I'd have socialized compliance requirements earlier — they nearly blocked launch. Now I lead with compliance architecture in every integration design._

#### The AI support chat

> Our support team was drowning — tickets piling up, same questions every week. I built an AI support chat using RAG on Pinecone + GPT-4, trained on our support docs. It **cut support tickets by 25%** and lead conversion went **up 15%** because prospects saw a product that could answer their questions instantly.

_Reflection: The RAG approach worked well, but I underestimated domain-tuning time. Next time I'd budget two weeks for answer-accuracy iteration before launch._

#### The agentic onboarding workflow

> Partner onboarding was a manual slog — **20+ touchpoints** per partner. I built an agentic workflow in n8n using OpenAI Assistants API that automated document collection, credential provisioning, and sandbox setup. **Cut manual touchpoints by ~60%** and dropped onboarding from weeks to days.

_Reflection: The agentic approach was powerful, but compliance steps needed a human-in-the-loop. The lesson was building that checkpoint pattern into the workflow design from day one._
